BWP (Boiling Water Proof) Marine Plywood adheres strictly to IS:710 standards. It is bonded with pure unextended synthetic Phenol Formaldehyde resin and can survive 72 hours of continuous boiling water immersion without delamination. It is compulsory for modular kitchens, washroom vanities, and wet areas. BWR (Boiling Water Resistant) adheres to IS:303 and survives 8 hours of boiling water, making it ideal for bedroom wardrobes, living room furniture, and wall units.
